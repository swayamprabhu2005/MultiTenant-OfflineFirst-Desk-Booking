# Office Presence - Who's in Office Specification

## Purpose
Provides real-time visibility into branch colleague presence and desk locations for workplace collaboration.

## Requirements
### Requirement: Branch-Scoped Colleague Presence
The system SHALL query active daily bookings and display colleague office presence.

#### Scenario: Viewing Colleague Presence
- **WHEN** clicking 'Who's in Office' on the dashboard or floor plan toolbar
- **THEN** a modal displays colleagues currently booked in the branch today, filtered by department with desk location tags.
